Features

Why it is different

Tamper-proof QR

ECDSA signature: a forged or edited QR is shown as fake instantly. No login needed to check.

Offline-first field app

Installable on any phone. Inspections are saved locally and sync automatically.

No manual maths

The officer types readings; the permissible error and verdict are calculated for them.

Fraud detection

GPS far from shop, rushed inspections, reused photos and more are flagged for review.

State dashboard

Pendency, district-wise status, officer workload, search and Excel export.

Complete audit trail

Every approval and change records who did it and when. Nothing changes quietly.
